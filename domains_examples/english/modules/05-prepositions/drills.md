# Упражнения

Вставь `in / on / at` или предлог движения/связки. Проверяй по образу «точка/
поверхность/объём».

## Блок 1. Место

```drill
type: fill-in
prompt: "The book is ___ the table."
answer: on
check: exact
```

```drill
type: fill-in
prompt: "She is ___ the kitchen."
answer: in
check: exact
```

```drill
type: fill-in
prompt: "Meet me ___ the bus stop."
answer: at
check: exact
```

```drill
type: multiple-choice
prompt: "«Я в автобусе»:"
options: [I am in the bus., I am on the bus., I am at the bus., I am into the bus.]
answer: I am on the bus.
check: exact
hint: транспорт, где ходишь → on
```

```drill
type: fill-in
prompt: "I live ___ Moscow."
answer: in
check: exact
```
