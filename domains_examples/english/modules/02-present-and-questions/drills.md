# Упражнения

Отрицания и вопросы с `do/does`, present continuous, выбор времени. Следи за помощником
и за тем, что после `does` глагол без `-s`.

## Блок 1. Отрицания

```drill
type: translate
prompt: "Переведи: Я не люблю кофе."
answer: I don't like coffee. | I do not like coffee.
check: fuzzy
```

```drill
type: fill-in
prompt: "She ___ live here. (отрицание глагола live)"
answer: doesn't | does not
check: fuzzy
hint: he/she/it → doesn't
```

```drill
type: multiple-choice
prompt: "Выбери правильное отрицание:"
options: [He doesn't works., He don't work., He doesn't work., He not work.]
answer: He doesn't work.
check: exact
hint: does → глагол голый
```

## Блок 5. Simple или Continuous?

```drill
type: multiple-choice
prompt: "«Я работаю в банке» (профессия, вообще):"
options: [I am working in a bank., I work in a bank., I working in a bank., I works in a bank.]
answer: I work in a bank.
check: exact
```

```drill
type: multiple-choice
prompt: "«Смотри! Идёт дождь» (прямо сейчас):"
options: [Look! It rains., Look! It is raining., Look! It rain., Look! Does it rain?]
answer: Look! It is raining.
check: exact
```

```drill
type: multiple-choice
prompt: "Какое предложение НЕправильное?"
options: [I know the answer., I am knowing the answer., She wants water., They are watching a film.]
answer: I am knowing the answer.
check: exact
hint: know — глагол состояния, не ставят в continuous
```

```drill
type: free-form
prompt: "Напиши 2 предложения: одно про себя «вообще» (present simple) и одно «прямо сейчас» (present continuous)."
answer: "Например: I usually drink tea. / Right now I am drinking coffee. (проверь: simple — work/works; continuous — am/is/are + -ing)"
check: manual
```
