# CLAUDE.md — drawing

Domain-specific instructions. Loaded after the root `CLAUDE.md` at the start of every
session in this domain. Keep it short and factual.

## Goal

Take the learner from "I can't draw" to **confidently sketching real objects, simple
scenes and basic figures from observation and from imagination** with a pencil: clean
lines, believable proportions, correct perspective, convincing light and shadow. The aim
is a sustainable daily sketching habit and the fundamentals that transfer to any medium
(ink, digital), not a specific style or "art career".

## Starting level

**Complete beginner.** Has not drawn since school. Believes drawing is a talent rather
than a trainable skill. Owns no materials yet. Adult, analytical mindset (programmer),
likes clear rules, feedback loops and measurable progress — and gets discouraged by vague
"just practice" advice.

Freeze this — progress goes into `memory.md`, not here.

## Methodology

- **Skill, not talent.** Drawing is decomposed into trainable sub-skills (seeing, line
  control, measuring, perspective, form, value, texture, figure, composition), each with a
  clear success criterion the learner can check themselves.
- **Seeing before drawing.** Module 01 follows the Betty Edwards insight: most beginner
  errors come from drawing *symbols* of what we know instead of *shapes* we see. Contour,
  negative space and upside-down exercises come first.
- **Construction over copying.** Objects are built from simple 3D forms (box, cylinder,
  sphere, cone) in perspective, then refined — the Loomis / Drawabox approach. Copying
  outlines without construction is discouraged.
- **Short daily sessions.** Every lesson is 20–40 minutes. Warm-ups (lines, ellipses,
  boxes) open each session. Volume of honest attempts beats perfection.
- **Feedback loop.** Each drill has a self-check rubric (3–5 yes/no questions). The
  learner photographs the result and, if wanted, describes it to Claude for feedback in
  words; `check: manual` everywhere.
- **Traditional pencil first.** Graphite on cheap paper is the default medium. Digital is
  mentioned as an optional path with the same fundamentals.
- **Mistakes named.** Every module lists the classic beginner errors (chicken-scratch lines,
  symbol eyes, floating shadows, ellipses with pointed ends, "tangents") and how to spot them.

## Preferred sources / authors

Real, verifiable references only:
- **Betty Edwards — "Drawing on the Right Side of the Brain"** (seeing, contour, negative space).
- **Andrew Loomis — "Fun with a Pencil"**, **"Figure Drawing for All It's Worth"**,
  **"Successful Drawing"** (construction, heads, figure; public-domain scans exist).
- **Bert Dodson — "Keys to Drawing"** (measuring, gesture, everyday practice).
- **Drawabox** — drawabox.com (free structured line/ellipse/box curriculum; the "50% rule").
- **Proko** — proko.com / YouTube (Stan Prokopenko: gesture, figure, head, shading).
- **Scott Robertson & Thomas Bertling — "How to Draw"** (perspective, technical construction).
- **Marshall Vandruff — "Perspective Drawing"** lectures (YouTube).
- **James Gurney — "Color and Light"** and gurneyjourney.blogspot.com (light, value; for later).
- **Marc Brunet / Sinix** channels as optional digital-side references.

## Explanation style

- Content in **Russian**; established English terms given once in parentheses
  («сердцевина тени (core shadow)», «жестовый рисунок (gesture)»).
- **Encouraging coach, concrete.** Every instruction says *what to do with the hand*, for
  how long, and what "good" looks like. No mysticism about talent or "feeling the form".
- Text cannot show pictures well, so: describe exercises step by step, use small ```text
  ASCII sketches for setups (horizon line, vanishing points, value scale, proportion grids),
  and point to the specific chapter/video of a real source for visuals. Suggest the learner
  save reference images into `misc/` and link them.
- ```mermaid only for process diagrams (session routine, construction order, decision
  "which perspective do I need").
- Each theory file: «Простыми словами» → зачем → как делать (пошагово) → чек-лист
  самопроверки → «Типичные ошибки» → «Словарь терминов» → «См. также».
- Cards: terms, principles, proportions, rules of thumb. Drills: `free-form`, `check: manual`,
  with a rubric in `answer:` and time-boxing in `prompt:` (e.g. «10 минут»).

## Out of scope

- **Color theory / painting / watercolor** — graphite and a little ink only; color is a
  later domain.
- **Anatomy in depth.** Figure is introduced at the proportions/gesture/mannequin level.
- **Digital tool tutorials** (Procreate/Photoshop UI). Fundamentals only.
- **Style, character design, comics** — mentioned as directions, not taught.

## Optional folders

- `misc/` — reference images, value scales, photos of the learner's work. Do not parse.
- `notes/` — user-owned `.md` notes managed by the UI. Do not write there unless asked.
